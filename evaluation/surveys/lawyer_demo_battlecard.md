## 2. Four Killer Live Demo Scenarios

Choose the query matching the lawyer's primary practice area:

### Scenario A: Property & Land Law (The Baina Trap)
* **Prompt to type:**  
  `"Can an unregistered agreement for sale executed in 2022 be specifically enforced in civil court?"`
* **What to highlight when Justor responds:**
  1. **Section 17A Registration Act & Section 54A Transfer of Property Act:** Compulsory registration requirement enacted by the 2004 amendment.
  2. **Section 21A Specific Relief Act:** Absolute statutory bar preventing civil courts from entertaining suits on unregistered contracts.
  3. **Supreme Court Precedent:** *Abdul Jalil vs Md. Joynal Abedin*, **67 DLR (AD) 142** with verbatim judicial ratio.

### Scenario B: Banking & Commercial Litigation (The NI Act 138 Notice Trap)
* **Prompt to type:**  
  `"Can a complainant file a Section 138 NI Act case 10 days after serving the legal notice?"`
* **What to highlight when Justor responds:**

  3. **Director Liability (§141):** Mention *Syed Farhad Hossain*, **70 DLR (AD) 140** for company cheques.

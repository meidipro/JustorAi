### Step 2: Realistic Non-Confidential Demonstration
*(Demonstrate a live research query in front of the lawyer. E.g.: "Can an unregistered agreement for sale be specifically enforced?" or "Predeceased grandson inheritance share under MFLO".)*

**Key Demonstration Elements to Show:**
1. **Governing Statute & Section:** Show exact section text with active/amended status.
2. **Recent Amendment Highlight:** Show Registration Act 2026 or Land Reforms 2023 status notes.
3. **Case Law Precedents:** Show relevant DLR / Supreme Court ruling with ratio decidendi.
4. **Instant Verification:** Click the **`PRIMARY SOURCE ✓`** and **`SOURCE CHECKED ✓`** official links directly in front of the lawyer to prove the citation is authentic.
5. **Acknowledge Gaps Openly:** If a section or case is missing, show how Justor explicitly states: *"This is not in my verified database — independent verification required"* rather than hallucinating.

# Employee Termination, Notice Pay & Severance under Bangladesh Labour Act

**Category:** Employment & Labour (চাকরি ও শ্রম আইন)  
**Applicable Law:** The Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006 (Act No. 42 of 2006, Sections 20, 26, 27, 28, 30, 33)  
**Verification:** PRIMARY SOURCE ✓ | SOURCE CHECKED ✓  
**Last Reviewed:** 15 August 2026  

---

## 1. Direct Legal Answer (At a Glance)
Under Section 26 of the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006, an employer can terminate the employment of a permanent worker without assigning any reason by giving **120 days’ written notice** (for monthly rated workers) or by paying basic wages in lieu of the notice period.

The terminated worker has an absolute statutory right to receive **compensation at the rate of 30 days’ wages for every completed year of service** (or gratuity, whichever is higher), in addition to payment for unavailed earned leave and provident fund dues.

---

## 2. Types of Separation & Statutory Entitlements

| Separation Type | Statutory Notice Period | Compensation / Severance Entitlement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Termination by Employer (Section 26)** | 120 Days (Monthly) / 60 Days (Other) | 30 days’ basic wage per completed year of service + Gratuity + Earned Leave encashment. |
| **Retrenchment due to Redundancy (Section 20)** | 1 Month written notice or notice pay | 30 days’ basic wage per completed year of service. |
| **Resignation by Employee (Section 27)** | 60 Days (Monthly) / 30 Days (Other) | 30 days’ wage per year if service $\ge$ 10 years; 15 days’ wage per year if service $\ge$ 5 to < 10 years. |
| **Dismissal for Misconduct (Section 23)** | Must follow formal charge sheet & enquiry | No severance compensation payable if guilt of theft/fraud/riot is proven. |

---

## 3. Mandatory Payment Timeline (Section 30)
Under Section 30 of the Labour Act, all wages, compensation, gratuity, and service benefits payable to a worker upon separation must be paid by the employer within **30 working days** from the date of cessation of employment.

---

## 4. Legal Remedy for Unlawful Termination (Section 33 Grievance Procedure)
1. **Written Grievance Petition:** Submit a written complaint to the employer within **30 days** of the termination or non-payment of benefits by registered post.
2. **Employer Response Window:** The employer has **15 days** to resolve the complaint and inform the worker in writing.
3. **Labour Court Lawsuit:** If the employer fails to respond within 15 days or rejects the grievance, the worker can file a formal case before the **Chairman, Labour Court** within the next **30 days**.

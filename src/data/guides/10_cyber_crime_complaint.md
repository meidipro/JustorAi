# How to File a Cyber Crime & Online Harassment Complaint in Bangladesh

**Category:** Cyber & Digital Law (সাইবার ও ডিজিটাল আইন)  
**Applicable Law:** The Cyber Security Act, 2023 (Act No. 27 of 2023); The Penal Code, 1860 (Sections 419, 500, 506, 509)

## 1. Direct Legal Answer (At a Glance)
If you are a victim of online blackmail, unauthorized circulation of private photos, identity theft (fake Facebook/Instagram ID), abusive cyber stalking, or financial fraud in Bangladesh, you have a statutory right to immediate police assistance.

Complaints can be lodged through:
1. **Police Cyber Support for Women (PCSW)**: Hotline `01320000888` / Email `pcsw@police.gov.bd` (100% confidential support for women and girls).
2. **CID Cyber Police Centre (CPC)**: Hotline `01769691522` / `cpc@police.gov.bd`.
3. **Local Police Station (Thana)**: Filing a formal General Diary (GD) or First Information Report (FIR).

---

## 2. Key Offenses & Statutory Penalties under Cyber Security Act, 2023

| Cyber Offense | Statutory Provision | Legal Consequence / Penalty |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Identity Theft / Fake Social Media Profiles** | Section 24, Cyber Security Act 2023 | Up to 5 years imprisonment or fine up to 5 Lakh BDT or both. |
| **Online Cheating & Financial Fraud** | Section 26, Cyber Security Act 2023 | Up to 5 years imprisonment or fine up to 5 Lakh BDT. |
| **Publication of Obscene or Defamatory Material** | Section 25 & 29, Cyber Security Act | Imprisonment and fine; immediate content takedown order to Meta/Google. |
| **Blackmail & Criminal Extortion** | Sections 383/384/506, Penal Code 1860 | Non-bailable criminal offense tried before Special Criminal Court. |

## 3. Step-by-Step Evidence Preservation & Filing Protocol
1. **Preserve Raw Digital Evidence:** Take clear screenshots of abusive messages, WhatsApp conversations, email headers, profile URLs, phone numbers, and payment transaction IDs (TrxID). **Do NOT delete the chat or block the offender before saving evidence.**
2. **Copy the Alphanumeric Account URL:** Do not just screenshot the display name. Copy the full link (e.g., `facebook.com/user.id.12345`).
3. **Submit to Cyber Support / Thana:** Lodge the complaint online or visit your local Thana with printed screenshots and your National ID card.
4. **Forensic Analysis & Takedown:** Police Cyber Units contact forensic labs and coordinate with Meta/Google/TikTok for emergency content removal and IP tracking.
5. **Cyber Tribunal Trial:** If the perpetrator is arrested, the case is tried before the specialized Divisional Cyber Tribunal.

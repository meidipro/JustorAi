# Muslim Inheritance & Property Distribution Rules in Bangladesh

**Category:** Family & Succession (পারিবারিক আইন)  
**Applicable Law:** Muslim Personal Law (Shariat) Application Act, 1937; Muslim Family Laws Ordinance, 1961 (Section 4); Partition Act, 1893  
**Verification:** PRIMARY SOURCE ✓ | SOURCE CHECKED ✓  
**Last Reviewed:** 15 August 2026  

---

## 1. Direct Legal Answer (At a Glance)
In Bangladesh, Muslim succession (Hanafi school) opens instantly upon the death of the property owner. After paying funeral costs, outstanding debts, and valid bequests (Wasiyat up to 1/3 of the net estate), the entire remaining property is divided among Quranic Sharers and Residuaries according to fixed statutory fractions.

Surviving sons inherit double the share of surviving daughters (2:1 ratio). Under **Section 4 of the Muslim Family Laws Ordinance, 1961**, children of a predeceased son or daughter inherit the exact share their parent would have received if alive at the time of succession.

## 2. Table of Fixed Primary Shares (Zawil-Furooz)

| Legal Heir | Condition / Family Scenario | Statutory Share Fraction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Wife (স্ত্রী)** | If husband left children or grandchildren | **1/8 (12.5%)** *(shared equally if multiple wives)* |
| **Wife (স্ত্রী)** | If husband left NO children or grandchildren | **1/4 (25%)** |
| **Husband (স্বামী)** | If wife left children or grandchildren | **1/4 (25%)** |
| **Husband (স্বামী)** | If wife left NO children or grandchildren | **1/2 (50%)** |
| **Mother (মাতা)** | If deceased left children or 2+ brothers/sisters | **1/6 (16.66%)** |
| **Mother (মাতা)** | If deceased left NO children and at most 1 sibling | **1/3 (33.33%)** |
| **Father (পিতা)** | If deceased left children | **1/6 (16.66%)** + Residue (if daughters only) |
| **Daughters (কন্যা)** | If single daughter and NO son | **1/2 (50%)** |
| **Daughters (কন্যা)** | If 2 or more daughters and NO son | **2/3 (66.66%)** divided equally |
| **Sons & Daughters** | If deceased left both sons and daughters | Sons & daughters take the remainder as **Residuaries in a 2:1 ratio** |

## 3. Inheritance of Orphaned Grandchildren (Section 4 MFLO 1961)
Under traditional pre-1961 Hanafi law, an orphaned grandchild was excluded if an uncle was alive. Section 4 of the Muslim Family Laws Ordinance, 1961 reformed this:
> *"In the event of the death of any son or daughter of the propositus before the opening of succession, the children of such son or daughter, if any, living at the time the succession opens, shall per stirpes receive a share equivalent to the share which such son or daughter, as the case may be, would have received if alive."*

---

## 4. How to Legally Partition Joint Inherited Property
1. **Warish Certificate:** Obtain the official legal heir certificate (ওয়ারিশান সনদ) from the local Union Parishad or City Corporation.
2. **Mutual Partition Deed (আপস বণ্টননামা দলিল):** Demarcate physical boundaries and register an amicable Partition Deed at the Sub-Registry office.
3. **Partition Suit in Civil Court (বাটোয়ারা মামলা):** If any co-sharer refuses to partition or unlawfully occupies excess land, file a Partition Suit under the Partition Act, 1893.

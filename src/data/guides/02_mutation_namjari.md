# Mutation (Namjari) Step-by-Step Guide / নামজারি কী ও কীভাবে করবেন

**Bangla Title:** ই-নামজারি (Mutation) কীভাবে করবেন: অনলাইনে আবেদন ও আইনি প্রক্রিয়া  
**English Subtitle:** E-Mutation Process, Khatian Creation, and Dispute Resolution in Bangladesh  
**Last Legally Reviewed:** 14 August 2026  
**Applicable Law:** The State Acquisition and Tenancy Act, 1950 (Sections 116, 117, 143, 144); The Land Reforms Ordinance, 1984; Ministry of Land E-Mutation Manual 2024–2026.

---

### Direct Answer (At a Glance)
Mutation (নামজারি / খারিজ) is the mandatory official administrative process of recording a new owner's name in the government land revenue record (Khatiyan) upon purchasing, inheriting, or receiving land via gift/hiba. Governed by Sections 117 and 143 of the State Acquisition and Tenancy Act, 1950, mutation separates the seller's joint record and creates a fresh Mutation Khatian (জমা খারিজ খতিয়ান) in the buyer's name. Without mutation, land development tax (Khajna) cannot be paid, and future title transfer is legally barred under Section 52A of the Registration Act.

* **Who this applies to:** New land purchasers, legal heirs, gift/hiba recipients, and auction buyers.
* **Official Application Portal:** [mutation.land.gov.bd](https://mutation.land.gov.bd) (Ministry of Land).
* **Official Statutory Fee:** Exactly **1,170 BDT** (Courffee: ৳20 + Notice fee: ৳50 + Record Correction fee: ৳1,000 + DCR fee: ৳100).

---

### What the Law Says (আইন কী বলে)
Under **Section 143 of the State Acquisition and Tenancy Act, 1950**, the Revenue Officer (Assistant Commissioner - Land / AC Land) is mandated to maintain and update the Record-of-Rights (ROR). Under **Section 117**, when a co-sharer transfers their specific share or a subdivision occurs, the holding is separated and a new annual land revenue rent-roll is established. Under **Section 52A of the Registration Act, 1908**, no Sub-Registrar shall register any sale deed unless the seller's name is recorded in the latest updated Khatian with proof of Land Development Tax payment.

---

### Step-by-Step E-Mutation Process
1. **Online Application:** Visit `mutation.land.gov.bd`, select your Division, District, Upazila, and Mouza, and enter applicant details (NID & mobile verification).
2. **Document Upload:** Upload scanned PDF/JPEG copies of the registered purchase deed, chain deeds (Bia Dalil), CS/SA/RS/BS Porcha, and succession certificate (if inheritance).
3. **Application Fee Payment:** Pay initial ৳70 (৳20 Court Fee + ৳50 Notice Fee) through Mobile Financial Services (bKash/Nagad/Rocket) or online banking.
4. **Union Land Office (Tahsilder) Verification:** The Union Land Assistant Officer (ULAO) inspects field possession, verifies revenue payment, and submits a digital inspection report to the AC (Land).
5. **Hearing & Document Presentation:** Appear for hearing at the Upazila AC (Land) office with original deeds and Khatians when notified via SMS.
6. **Approval & DCR Payment:** Upon AC (Land) approval, pay the final ৳1,100 (৳1,000 Khatian correction + ৳100 DCR fee) online.
7. **Download Digital Khatian & DCR:** Instantly download and print the QR-code verified Mutation Khatian and Duplicate Carbon Receipt (DCR).

---

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
1. **Paying Cash to Intermediaries (Dalals):** All official mutation fees must be paid directly via the online portal. Never hand cash to brokers.
2. **Applying Without Up-to-Date Khajna:** Applications without the current financial year's Land Development Tax receipt are automatically rejected by the Union Land Office.
3. **Mismatched Mouza & Plot Numbers:** Ensure the Dag (Plot) and Khatian numbers on the purchase deed match the RS/BS survey records perfectly.

### Where to Apply or Complain
* **Official Portal:** [mutation.land.gov.bd](https://mutation.land.gov.bd)
* **Helpline:** Ministry of Land Citizen Call Center: **16122** (24/7 hotline).
* **Appeals & Dispute Forum:** If an AC (Land) rejects an application unlawfully, an appeal lies before the **Additional Deputy Commissioner - Revenue (ADC Revenue)** within 30 days under Section 147 of the SAT Act, 1950.

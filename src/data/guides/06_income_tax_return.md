# How to File Income Tax e-Return in Bangladesh (2026–27)

**Category:** Tax & Finance (কর ও অর্থ)  
**Applicable Law:** The Income Tax Act, 2023 (Act No. 12 of 2023, Sections 166, 171, 264); NBR e-Return Rules

**Last Reviewed:** 15 August 2026

## 1. Direct Legal Answer (At a Glance)
Under Section 166 of the Income Tax Act, 2023, every individual whose total income during the income year exceeds the maximum amount not chargeable to tax (350,000 BDT for general individuals; 400,000 BDT for female and senior citizens aged 65+) or who falls under mandatory filing criteria under Section 264 must submit an annual return of income on or before Tax Day (30th November).

The National Board of Revenue (NBR) mandates digital submission through the official portal: **etaxnbr.gov.bd**.

---

## 2. Who Must File (Mandatory PSR Categories under Section 264)
Even if your taxable income is zero, submitting an income tax return and obtaining **Proof of Submission of Return (PSR)** is mandatory for:
1. Obtaining a bank loan exceeding 500,000 BDT.
2. Opening or renewing a business Trade License.
3. Purchasing or renewing registration/fitness for a motor vehicle.
4. Obtaining or renewing membership of professional bodies (Bar Council, Medical Council, Engineers, Accountants).
5. Buying or selling immovable property located in City Corporations, Paurashavas, or Cantonments where deed value exceeds 10 Lakh BDT.
6. Getting new electricity, gas, or commercial utility connections.

## 3. Current Individual Tax Slabs (2026–27 Assessment Year)

| Total Income Threshold | Applicable Tax Rate |
| :--- | :--- |
| First 350,000 BDT *(400,000 BDT for female/seniors)* | **0% (Nil)** |
| Next 100,000 BDT | **5%** |
| Next 400,000 BDT | **10%** |
| Next 500,000 BDT | **15%** |
| Next 500,000 BDT | **20%** |
| On remaining balance | **25%** |

*Minimum Tax:* Dhaka/Chittagong City Corporation = 5,000 BDT; Other City Corporations = 4,000 BDT; Other Areas = 3,000 BDT.

## 4. Step-by-Step e-Return Submission Procedure
1. **Online Registration:** Visit `etaxnbr.gov.bd`, register with your 12-digit e-TIN and biometric NID-linked mobile number.
2. **Income Heads Entry:** Declare salaries, rental income, business profits, agricultural income, capital gains, and financial interest.
3. **Investment Tax Rebate:** Enter eligible investments (Part 3, Sixth Schedule: DPS up to 1.2 Lakh BDT, Life Insurance, Treasury Bonds, Sanchayapatra) to get a 15% statutory tax rebate.
4. **Statement of Assets and Liabilities (IT-10B):** Enter gross assets, motor vehicles, gold, bank balances as of 30th June, and family expenditure.
5. **e-Payment & Instant PSR:** Pay net tax liability via online banking/MFS and download your system-generated PSR Acknowledgement Receipt.

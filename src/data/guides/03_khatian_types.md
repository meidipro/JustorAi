# Understanding Khatian Types: CS, SA, RS, BS / খতিয়ান কী ও চেনার উপায়

**Bangla Title:** CS, SA, RS ও BS খতিয়ান চেনার উপায় ও আইনি গুরুত্ব  
**English Subtitle:** Comprehensive Guide to Bangladesh Land Record of Rights (Porcha / Khatian)  
**Last Legally Reviewed:** 14 August 2026  
**Applicable Law:** The Bengal Tenancy Act, 1885; The State Acquisition and Tenancy Act, 1950 (Section 144); The Survey Act, 1875.

---

### Direct Answer (At a Glance)
A Khatian (খতিয়ান), also legally termed the Record of Rights (ROR / স্বত্বলিপি), is the official public document prepared by government land survey authorities specifying land ownership, boundary shares, plot (Dag) numbers, class of land, and annual revenue payable. In Bangladesh, four major historical surveys exist: **CS (Cadastral Survey, 1888–1940)**, **SA (State Acquisition Survey, 1956–1962)**, **RS (Revisional Survey, 1965–1990s)**, and **BS / City Survey (Bangladesh Survey, 1998–Present)**. Correctly matching the chain of title across all four surveys is essential to prove indefeasible legal ownership.

* **Who this applies to:** Property buyers, civil litigators, deed writers, and financial institutions conducting title searches.
* **Official Verification Portal:** [eporcha.gov.bd](https://eporcha.gov.bd) (Directorate of Land Records and Surveys).
* **Legal Presumption:** A recorded entry in the latest published ROR carries a statutory presumption of correctness in civil court under Section 144(4) of the SAT Act, 1950.

---

### Comparison of the 4 Major Khatian Types

| Khatian Type | Full Name & Survey Era | Distinguishing Features & Structure | Legal Significance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **CS Khatian** | **Cadastral Survey** (1888–1940) | Two-page landscape format; lists Zamindar/Landlord name on top and Praja (tenant) name below. | Baseline historical root of all land titles in Bangladesh. |
| **SA Khatian** | **State Acquisition Survey** (1956–1962) | Single-sheet format based on Zamindari Abolition; shows former Zamindar compensation and tenant direct ownership. | Proof of title when Zamindari rent-seeking was legally abolished under SAT Act 1950. |
| **RS Khatian** | **Revisional Survey** (1965–1990s) | Portrait format with explicit column division; corrected massive clerical errors from rapid SA survey. | Highly authoritative in civil litigation across most districts outside Dhaka/Chittagong. |
| **BS / City Khatian** | **Bangladesh Survey / City Survey** (1998–Present) | Modern digitized format with computerized Dag numbers; current active revenue record in metropolitan areas. | Primary survey record currently enforced by Sub-Registry and AC (Land) offices. |

---

### How to Verify a Khatian's Authenticity
1. **Physical Inspection:** Look for the official watermark, circular seal of the Deputy Commissioner (DC Record Room), serial volume number, and authorized signature.
2. **Online E-Porcha Verification:** Visit [eporcha.gov.bd](https://eporcha.gov.bd), enter the District, Upazila, Mouza, Survey Type (RS/BS), and Khatian Number to view certified digital extracts.
3. **Chain of Ownership Check:** Ensure the name in the RS Khatian logically traces back to the inheritance or transfer deed of the person recorded in the SA Khatian.

---

### Common Khatian Errors & Legal Remedies
* **Clerical/Typographical Errors (নাম বা হিস্যার ভুল):** Apply directly to the Upazila AC (Land) under Section 143/144 for record correction.
* **Fraudulent Recording (ভুল রেকর্ড / অন্যের নামে রেকর্ড):** If land is erroneously recorded in a stranger's name during BS survey, the aggrieved party must file a suit in the **Land Survey Tribunal (LST)** or Civil Court for declaration of title under Section 42 of the Specific Relief Act, 1877 within the statutory limitation period.

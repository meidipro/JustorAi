# How to Claim Denmohor (Dower) & Child Maintenance in Family Court

**Category:** Family & Succession (পারিবারিক আইন)  
**Applicable Law:** The Family Courts Act, 2023 (Act No. 8 of 2023); Muslim Family Laws Ordinance, 1961  
**Verification:** PRIMARY SOURCE ✓ | SOURCE CHECKED ✓  
**Last Reviewed:** 15 August 2026  

---

## 1. Direct Legal Answer (At a Glance)
In Bangladesh, Denmohor (দেনমোহর / Dower) is the absolute and unconditional financial debt of the husband to the wife stipulated in the Kabinnama (Nikahnama). The husband remains legally bound to pay 100% of the agreed dower regardless of whether the marriage is subsisting or dissolved, and regardless of who initiated the divorce.

Under Section 5 of the **Family Courts Act, 2023**, the Family Court (পারিবারিক আদালত) has exclusive original jurisdiction to decree dower, past and future child maintenance, and restitution of conjugal rights.

---

## 2. Prompt vs Deferred Dower: Recovery Rules & Limitation

| Dower Type | When It Becomes Payable | Statutory Limitation Window |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Prompt Dower (মোয়াজ্জল)** | Exigible immediately upon demand at any time during marriage. | **3 Years** from the date of formal demand and husband’s refusal (Article 103, Limitation Act, 1908). |
| **Deferred Dower (মুয়াজ্জল)** | Payable upon dissolution of marriage by divorce or upon death of either spouse. | **3 Years** from the date of dissolution of marriage or death (Article 104, Limitation Act, 1908). |

---

## 3. Child Maintenance (সন্তানের ভরণপোষণ)
* A Muslim father has an absolute, inalienable legal obligation to maintain his biological children according to his financial status.
* Custody (হিফাযত / Hizanat) of minor children by the divorced mother does not relieve the father of his duty to pay regular monthly child maintenance (food, shelter, medical, clothing, and education expenses).
* Under Section 16 of the Family Courts Act 2023, the Family Court can grant **interim maintenance (অন্তর্র্বতীকালীন ভরণপোষণ)** from the first month of filing before final judgment.

---

## 4. Court Procedure & Execution
1. **Filing Plaint:** The wife files a suit in the local Family Court (Court of Assistant Judge) where she resides or where marriage took place. Fixed court fee is strictly 50 BDT.
2. **Pre-Trial Conciliation:** The Judge holds mandatory private conciliation under Section 10.
3. **Trial & Decree:** If conciliation fails, evidence is taken and the court decrees the outstanding dower and monthly child maintenance.
4. **Execution (Jari Case):** If the husband fails to deposit the decreed amount, the court issues distress warrants, attaches properties/salary, or sentences the husband to civil imprisonment under Section 19.

---

## 5. Primary Statutory References
* **The Family Courts Act, 2023 (Act No. 8 of 2023)**: Sections 4, 5, 10, 16, 19.
* **The Muslim Family Laws Ordinance, 1961**: Section 9 (Maintenance) & Section 10 (Dower).
* **The Limitation Act, 1908**: Schedule I, Articles 103 and 104.

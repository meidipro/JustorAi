# How to Register Land in Bangladesh / জমি নিবন্ধন কীভাবে করবেন

**Bangla Title:** জমি নিবন্ধন কীভাবে করবেন: ধাপে ধাপে সরকারি নিয়ম ও প্রক্রিয়া  
**English Subtitle:** Step-by-Step Guide to Land Registration & Stamp Duty in Bangladesh  
**Last Legally Reviewed:** 14 August 2026  
**Applicable Law:** The Registration Act, 1908 (including Registration (Amendment) Act, 2026, Act No. 14 of 2026); The Transfer of Property Act, 1882; The Stamp Act, 1899.

---

### Direct Answer (At a Glance)
In Bangladesh, transferring immovable property valued above 100 BDT or executing an agreement for sale requires mandatory written registration under Section 17 of the Registration Act, 1908 and Section 54 of the Transfer of Property Act, 1882. Registration must be completed at the Sub-Registry office within the local jurisdiction where the land is situated within the statutory time limits. Failure to register renders the sale void and legally unenforceable in court.

* **Who this applies to:** Land buyers, sellers, deed writers (দলিল লেখক), and property heirs.
* **Statutory Deadline:** Presentation within 3 months under Section 23 of Registration Act (subject to 2026 Amendment Section 17A rules).
* **Primary Authority:** Sub-Registry Office (Registration Directorate, Ministry of Law).

---

### What the Law Says (আইন কী বলে)
Under **Section 17(1) of the Registration Act, 1908**, all non-testamentary instruments transferring or creating any right, title, or interest in immovable property must be registered. Under **Section 54 of the Transfer of Property Act, 1882**, a contract for the sale of immovable property does not create any interest or charge on such property without a registered instrument (Baina-Nama or Kabala). Under **Section 49 of the Registration Act**, an unregistered document cannot affect any immovable property or be received as evidence of any transaction affecting such property.

---

### Step-by-Step Registration Process
1. **Title & Ownership Verification:** Verify the seller's CS, SA, RS, and City/BS Khatians, updated Namjari (Mutation), and Land Development Tax (Khajna) receipts.
2. **Deed Drafting:** Draft the registered deed (Saf-Kabala / Baina-Nama) on official non-judicial stamp papers through a certified Deed Writer or Advocate.
3. **Valuation & Stamp Duty Assessment:** Calculate stamp duty, registration fee, local government tax (LGED/Pourashava/City Corporation), source tax (AIT), and gain tax based on official Mouza rates.
4. **Bank Pay Order / E-Payment:** Deposit all government duties and taxes via treasury challan or designated bank pay orders.
5. **Presentation at Sub-Registry Office:** Buyer and seller appear physically before the Sub-Registrar with National ID cards (NID), biometrics, photographs, and witnesses.
6. **Execution & Endorsement:** The Sub-Registrar verifies identity, consideration payment, takes digital fingerprints, and issues the temporary receipt (I-Receipt).
7. **Original Deed Collection:** Upon completion of volume indexing, collect the original laminated deed by surrendering the I-Receipt.

### Required Documents Checklist
- [x] Certified copy of latest Mutation Khatian (নামজারি খতিয়ান) & DCR.
- [x] Up-to-date Land Development Tax (ভূমি উন্নয়ন কর / খাজনা রশিদ).
- [x] CS, SA, RS, and BS Record of Rights (Porcha/Khatian chain).
- [x] Bia Deeds (বায়া দলিল / Chain of previous title deeds for past 25 years).
- [x] NID cards and passport-size photographs of buyer, seller, and identifier.
- [x] Non-encumbrance certificate (NEC) from Sub-Registry office (recommended).

---

### Official Government Fees & Timelines
* **Stamp Duty:** 1.5% of deed valuation.
* **Registration Fee:** 1% of deed valuation.
* **Local Government Tax:** 2% – 3% (depending on Union Parishad / Pourashava / City Corporation).
* **Source Tax (AIT):** Varies by commercial/residential zoning and Mouza rate schedule.
* **Processing Timeline:** 1 day for execution & biometric endorsement; original deed delivery within 30–90 days.

### Real-Life Scenario
> **Scenario:** Rahim agreed to purchase a 5-katha plot from Karim in Savar for 30 Lakh BDT. Rahim paid 10 Lakh BDT advance on an oral promise without executing a registered agreement. Two months later, Karim sold the same plot to another buyer via a registered Saf-Kabala deed.  
> **Legal Outcome:** Under Section 17A of the Registration Act and Section 21A of the Specific Relief Act, Rahim's unregistered oral agreement is legally invalid. The second registered buyer holds lawful title. Rahim can only file a money suit to recover the paid advance.

---

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
1. **Relying on Unregistered Baina-Nama:** An unregistered agreement for sale has zero legal validity for specific performance under Section 21A of Specific Relief Act.
2. **Ignoring Mouza Value Under-declaration:** Under-declaring value to evade stamp duty risks penal seizure and valuation disputes under the Stamp Act.
3. **Failing to Verify Chain Deeds (Bia Dalil):** Registering land without examining the unbroken 25-year title chain leaves the buyer vulnerable to hidden heirs or mortgage claims.

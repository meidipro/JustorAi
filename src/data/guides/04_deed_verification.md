# How to Verify a Land Deed (Dalil) in Bangladesh / দলিল যাচাইয়ের আইনি উপায়

**Bangla Title:** দলিল আসল না ভুয়া কীভাবে যাচাই করবেন: জাল দলিল শনাক্তের ৫টি ধাপ  
**English Subtitle:** Step-by-Step Legal Verification of Title Deeds & Non-Encumbrance in Bangladesh  
**Last Legally Reviewed:** 14 August 2026  
**Applicable Law:** The Registration Act, 1908 (Sections 57, 60); The Penal Code, 1860 (Sections 463, 464, 467, 471 on Forgery); The Land Crime Prevention and Redress Act, 2023.

---

### Direct Answer (At a Glance)
A title deed (দলিল) in Bangladesh is legally authentic only when its execution, volume indexing, thumbprints, and seal correspond exactly to the permanent records preserved in the District/Sub-Registry Record Room (বালাম বই). Under Section 57 of the Registration Act, 1908, any member of the public can apply for a formal inspection (Talashi) or certified copy of any registered document upon paying the statutory fee. Forged or cloned deeds can be criminally prosecuted under the Land Crime Prevention and Redress Act, 2023, carrying rigorous imprisonment up to 7 years.

* **Who this applies to:** Property buyers, banks, heirs, and landowners suspecting fraudulent transfers.
* **Primary Authority:** Sub-Registry Office & District Registrar Record Room.
* **Verification Instrument:** Non-Encumbrance Certificate (NEC / এন.ই.সি) and Balam Search.

---

### 5-Step Deed Verification Checklist

1. **Volume & Register Inspection (বালাম বই তল্লাশি):**
   - Apply at the concerned Sub-Registry office under **Section 57 of the Registration Act, 1908** with Form 36 to inspect the Balam volume corresponding to the deed number, volume number, and year.
   - Verify that the names of parties, plot schedule, consideration money, and Sub-Registrar's signature match the original deed verbatim.

2. **Stamp Paper & Cartridge Paper Authenticity:**
   - Inspect the serial numbers printed on the non-judicial stamp papers. Check the vendor license number and date of stamp purchase printed on the reverse side. If stamp papers were purchased after the alleged date of registration, the deed is a counterfeit.

3. **Sub-Registrar Endorsement Seal (পৃষ্ঠাংকন ও সিল):**
   - Under **Section 60 of the Registration Act**, the Sub-Registrar must affix a certificate containing the book number, volume number, and page number, signed with date and seal.

4. **Chain Deed (বায়া দলিল) Correlation:**
   - Examine whether the seller acquired the property via a valid previous title deed (Bia Dalil) and whether that prior deed is recorded in the Balam books for the past 25 continuous years.

5. **Physical Possession & Local Tahsil Inquiries:**
   - Visit the local Union Land Office to ensure no mutation has occurred under another party's name and verify physical possession on the ground.

---

### Penalties for Forged Deeds under Bangladeshi Law
* **Land Crime Prevention and Redress Act, 2023 (Section 4):** Manufacturing or using forged land documents is punishable by imprisonment for up to **7 years and monetary fines**.
* **Penal Code, 1860 (Sections 467 & 471):** Forgery of valuable security carries imprisonment for life or up to **10 years**.
* **Civil Remedy:** The aggrieved rightful owner can file a suit for **cancellation of forged deed** under Section 39 of the Specific Relief Act, 1877 within 3 years of discovering the fraud.

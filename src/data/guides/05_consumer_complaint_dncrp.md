# How to File a Consumer Complaint in Bangladesh (DNCRP Guide)

**Bangla Title:** ভোক্তা অধিকার অধিদপ্তরে অভিযোগ করার সহজ নিয়ম ও ক্ষতিপূরণ পাওয়ার উপায়  
**English Subtitle:** Step-by-Step DNCRP Consumer Complaint Filing, Evidence Preservation & 25% Compensation  
**Last Legally Reviewed:** 14 August 2026  
**Applicable Law:** The Consumer Rights Protection Act, 2009 (Act No. 26 of 2009); National Consumer Rights Protection Rules.

---

### Direct Answer (At a Glance)
Under Section 76(1) of the **Consumer Rights Protection Act, 2009**, any individual who suffers from deceptive trade practices, defective products, adulterated goods, overcharging above MRP, misleading advertisements, or unfulfilled paid e-commerce services can file a formal complaint before the **Directorate of National Consumer Rights Protection (DNCRP / জাতীয় ভোক্তা অধিকার সংরক্ষণ অধিদপ্তর)** within **30 days** of the occurrence. If the administrative complaint results in a statutory fine against the offender, the complainant is entitled by law to receive **25% of the collected fine** as an immediate cash reward under Section 76(4).

* **Who this applies to:** Consumers, online e-commerce shoppers, retail buyers, service recipients, and patients.
* **Official Hotline:** **16121** (DNCRP 24/7 Consumer Call Center).
* **Statutory Filing Window:** Exactly **30 calendar days** from the date of purchase or cause of action.

---

### Grounds for a Valid Consumer Complaint (Section 37–53)
1. **Selling above Maximum Retail Price (MRP):** Charging higher than the printed package price (Section 40).
2. **Defective or Adulterated Goods:** Selling expired, fake, or hazardous products (Sections 41, 42).
3. **Deceptive E-Commerce Practices:** Failing to deliver ordered products after taking advance payment or delivering mismatched counterfeits (Section 45).
4. **False or Misleading Advertisement:** Publishing deceptive promotional claims (Section 44).
5. **Short Weight / Measurement:** Supplying less quantity than represented (Sections 46, 47).

---

### Step-by-Step DNCRP Complaint Filing Process
1. **Preserve Concrete Evidence:** Immediately save physical purchase receipts/cash memos, product photos, warranty cards, screenshots of e-commerce order confirmation, payment transaction IDs (bKash/Nagad), and courier delivery slips.
2. **Draft the Complaint:** Write a plain letter or email addressed to the Director General of DNCRP or Deputy Director of your local division stating:
   - Full name, mobile number, NID, and address of the complainant.
   - Exact business name, physical store address, website/Facebook page link, and contact number of the seller.
   - Chronological summary of what happened, dates, amount paid, and specific harm suffered.
3. **Submit via Official Channels:**
   - **Email:** Submit scanned complaint and evidence to `dg@dncrp.gov.bd` or `ncrp.complaint@gmail.com`.
   - **Online Portal:** File directly at [dncrp.portal.gov.bd](https://dncrp.portal.gov.bd).
   - **Physical Submission:** Submit in person or via registered post to the Central or District DNCRP office.
4. **Hearing & Mediation:** Both complainant and the merchant are summoned for an administrative hearing. The officer examines physical samples and transaction proofs.
5. **Order & 25% Compensation:** Upon proving the offense, the magistrate/officer levies an administrative fine (up to ৳2,00,000) and orders the merchant to refund the customer, immediately handing over 25% of the penalty amount to the complainant.

---

### What You Must NOT Do
* **Do Not Delay Beyond 30 Days:** Complaints filed after 30 days of purchase are legally time-barred under Section 76(1) and will be dismissed without hearing.
* **Do Not File Without Proof of Purchase:** Without a cash memo, invoice, payment slip, or digital chat receipt, the complaint lacks legal standing.
